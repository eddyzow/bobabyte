Ready for a bobatastic adventure of creativity?

BobaByte is an social coding event for students in grades 7-12. Everyone from newbies to experienced coders are invited! Projects will be judged on many categories, so everyone has a chance to win awesome prizes. Free food, merch, and boba will be served!

Open to all.

We'd especially love to see students who've never attended a social coding event (i.e. hackathon) before. We'll be offering numerous coding workshops and mentoring students as they code. There's even an award specifically dedicated to newbies!

A dedicated team.

The BobaByte 2025 team attended the original Bobabyte 2024, and are hoping to share how that experience helped foster our love for computer science!

Everything's free.

Food, merch, and boba served during the day are all fully covered by our amazing sponsors. You can even get reimbursement for travel to BobaByte!

Amazing prizes.

Judging categories are broad and range from Fan Favorite all the way to Judges' Choice. There's a category for everyone! Top winners will receive all sorts of prizes, and everyone who shows up gets a free .xyz domain!

About the Team Behind BobaByte

BobaByte is run by students of Acton-Boxborough Regional High School in Acton, MA. We believe that with today's world becoming increasingly digital, it is crucial for youth to become well-versed in programming for success in the modern workforce. As attendees of Bobabyte 2024, we have firsthand experience of the effectiveness of the hackathon in supporting our interest!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Who can participate?

Anyone in grades 7-12!

How much does it cost?

Free! Our generous sponsors will fund all merchandise, food, boba, and prizes. Hack Club's Gas Fund may even cover travel costs.

Who can I work with?

Teams of up to 4 people are accepted! You can make new friends when you arrive, bring your own, or work alone!

What can I make?

Code any project you can make within the eight hours of BobaByte! A video game, a webapp, a Scratch project - the possibilities are endless.

What are the prizes?

Prizes include gift cards, gaming keyboards, boba plushes, board games, and more! All participants will also take home a free .xyz domain!

How do I win?

We will be giving out awards to every member of teams that win in three categories: Fan Favorite, Judges' Choice, Most Technical, and Most Creative. For the Fan Favorite category, you'll vote on which project wins the award! Judges' Choice and Most Creative will be selected by the judges.

What's a hackathon?

A traditional hackathon is an event where teams "hack" (code projects) for a period of time, often 24 hours. These projects are then judged, and the winners receive prizes. BobaByte is like a mini-hackathon, but we especially emphasize the learning aspect. We will be hosting workshops throughout the day to help young people of all skill levels.

I've never coded before!

This social coding event is about teaching people how awesome coding is. Our high school programming leads, experienced in Java, Python, and Scratch, can help guide you in the right direction!
